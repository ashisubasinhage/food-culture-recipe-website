Food Culture

Understanding Food Culture

Food culture is the way people choose, prepare, share, and enjoy food within a community. It reflects a country's history, traditions, climate, local ingredients, celebrations, and everyday lifestyle.

Exploring food culture helps us understand how food connects people and how traditional dishes and cooking practices are passed from one generation to another.
